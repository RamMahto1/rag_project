Company Policy

Working Hours:
Employees work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Annual Leave:
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year.

Remote Work:
Employees can work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Sick Leave:
Employees must inform their manager when they are unable to work because of illness.

Office Attendance:
Employees are expected to attend the office on their scheduled office days.

Performance Review:
Employee performance is reviewed twice a year.

Training:
Employees are encouraged to complete at least 20 hours of professional training each year.

Information Security:
Employees must keep company information confidential and must not share sensitive company data with unauthorized people.
